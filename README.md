# IDBLOOM BPAHK Presentation Brief

This repo contains the IDBLOOM demo and strategy notes for the BPA global competition.

## Topic

**Topic 3: Labor Identity — How Digital Technology Turns Personal Uniqueness into Economic Value**

Competition format:

- Slides are required.
- Presentation: 7-minute group presentation.
- Q&A: 2 minutes in preliminary round, 4 minutes in final round.
- AI use must be acknowledged in the slides.

## Core Idea

**IDBLOOM** is an AI identity companion for homemakers.

It helps homemakers turn invisible home expertise into visible economic value.

Core formula:

```text
IDBLOOM = Inner Identity + Demand Delivery
```

- **Inner Identity**: AI discovers hidden skills from daily home life.
- **Demand Delivery**: AI packages those skills into services, content, products, and market opportunities.

One-line pitch:

```text
IDBLOOM turns invisible home expertise into visible economic value.
```

## Main Insight

Homemakers are not lacking ability. Their ability is simply not named, recorded, priced, or distributed.

Use this framing:

1. They are not skill-less. Their skills are unnamed.
2. They are not workless. Their work is unpaid and unseen.
3. They are not without identity. They lack a digital translator between home experience and market language.

## Product Flow

```text
Daily life input
  -> hidden skill detection
  -> Identity Passport
  -> service/content/product packaging
  -> platform matching
  -> income and feedback
```

Example:

```text
"I used 6 storage boxes to reorganize my kitchen."

AI output:
- Space Planning
- Storage System Design
- Child Independence Training
- Family Workflow Optimization

Market output:
- Small Kitchen Reset Session
- Xiaohongshu post
- TikTok script
- Local parent group offer
```

## Q&A Defense

Likely questions:

- Why homemakers?
  - Because their work is valuable but systematically under-recorded and under-priced.

- How is this different from a home service platform?
  - Home service platforms match labor to orders. IDBLOOM first builds identity, evidence, and professional packaging.

- What about privacy?
  - Recording is consent-based. Sensitive information is hidden. Users confirm before anything is published.

- How does it make money?
  - Subscription, commission, B2B partnerships, and certification courses.

- Is AI generating the core work?
  - No. AI supports brainstorming, wording, mockup generation, and demo simulation. The team owns the core idea and final adaptation.
